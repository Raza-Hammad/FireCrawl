## Frequently Asked Questions

Can we use this across multiple branches?

Yes. Multi-branch management with centralized revenue reporting is fully supported on the Pro and Enterprise plans.

Can receptionists use this in Urdu?

Yes. The receptionist desk and patient WhatsApp flows support both English and Roman Urdu / Urdu layouts.

How do doctors receive their commission payouts?

Settlements generated automatically at midnight (12:00 AM) or tailored to match your clinic's evening OPD closing hours. You can settle in cash or direct bank transfer, complete with downloadable PDF reconciliations.

What happens if our clinic internet goes down during OPD?

The counter token queue operates with offline fallback so patient numbers don't halt. Data automatically syncs the moment connectivity returns.

How does WhatsApp booking work?

Patients start a conversation through your clinic's WhatsApp, select the relevant doctor, and choose an available appointment slot instantly.

What happens after a visit?

Visit information, prescriptions, follow-up instructions and lab reports are instantly sent to the patient's WhatsApp profile.

Can I export our patient and revenue data if we stop using PatientCare?

Yes. You retain 100% ownership of your clinic data. You can download complete patient directories and billing histories in Excel or PDF formats at any time.

- **Consultation & Token Data:** Appointment timestamps, queue status, digital token numbers, diagnosis notes, prescription archives, and fee/billing splits.
